The basics

Every time you hit Generate New Card, you get a random 5×5 card dealt from the squares in bingo_squares.json.

  • 24 random squares are used; the center tile is a free space.
  • Click or tap any square to mark or unmark it as you hear it in the demo.
  • Refreshing the page or generating a new card clears your marks.

Pro tip: mirror this tab on the meeting screen or share the link so the whole team can play along.

Want to add your own squares? Edit bingo_squares.json in the repo. It's a plain list of strings, and at least 24 are needed.
